In January the man in charge of China’s tax department took the stage at an annual conference in Beijing alongside a group of Communist Party officials to lay out the year’s priorities. [Hu Jinglin](bbg://people/profile/17521676), donning thin glasses and a military-style crew cut, began with the usual catchphrases of his country’s politics: [Xi Jinping](bbg://people/profile/4648789) thought; socialism with Chinese characteristics; the spirit of the 20th National Congress. But this wasn’t a typical speech. Hu then promised a new era of taxation for the $21 trillion economy.  
1 月，中国税务部门负责人与一群共产党官员在北京的一次年度会议上登台，阐述了当年的工作重点。戴着细框眼镜、留着军人式平头的胡静林，一开始就提到了中国政治中的常见口号：习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想；中国特色社会主义；二十大精神。但这并非一场寻常的讲话。胡静林随后承诺，将为这个 21 万亿美元规模的经济体开启税收新时代。

So far, he is proving to be a man of his word.  
迄今为止，他正在兑现自己的承诺。

Across China, tax officials have gone into overdrive to boost receipts from the country’s richest people and biggest companies. They’ve rewritten the rules on [offshore trusts](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/china-targets-offshore-trusts-in-sweeping-tax-clampdown), launched the biggest raid for back taxes on [local companies](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/xi-s-unpredecented-tax-clawback-hammers-chinese-listed-companies) in years and set their sights on the more than $1.7 trillion of wealth that Chinese residents hold in Hong Kong alone.  
全国各地的税务部门正全力加强对富豪及大型企业的税收征管。他们重写了离岸信托规则，开展了多年来最大规模的企业补税稽查行动，并将目光投向中国居民仅在香港就持有的超过 1.7 万亿美元资产。

![](https://assets.bwbx.io/images/users/iqjWHBFdfxIU/iytwbpkmd6Sk/v3/pidjEfPlU1QWZop3vfGKsrX.ke8XuWirGYh1PKgEw44kE/-1x-1.png)

They’ve also formed a series of special squads dedicated to tracking and taxing the rich, with some of these units focused on just one billionaire, according to people familiar with the matter. These teams are a counterforce to the army of lawyers, tax advisers and consultants China’s megarich hire to lower their tax bills. They bring together local officials in cities or provinces, inspection experts, and even regulators, the people say.  
据知情人士透露，他们还组建了一系列专门针对富豪的稽查小组，其中部分团队甚至只盯防一位亿万富翁。这些团队旨在对抗中国超级富豪们聘请的律师、税务顾问和咨询师大军——后者专门帮助富豪降低税负。知情人士称，稽查小组由各市县官员、稽查专家甚至监管机构成员联合组成。

Since China joined the World Trade Organization in December 2001, the average income of its citizens has swelled from just over $1,000 to more than $14,000 today. Hundreds of newly minted billionaires have emerged, eager to splash their cash on European art auctions, London property deals and US capital markets. Now the gravy train is being derailed, according to dozens of interviews with tax advisers, lawyers, bankers and high-net-worth investors. They asked to speak on condition of anonymity because many of the details remain private. China’s state tax department didn’t respond to requests for comment.  
自 2001 年 12 月加入世界贸易组织以来，中国国民平均收入已从 1000 美元飙升至如今的 1.4 万美元。数百位新晋亿万富豪随之涌现，热衷于在欧洲艺术品拍卖会、伦敦房地产交易和美国资本市场挥金如土。但通过对税务顾问、律师、银行家及高净值投资者的数十次访谈发现，这场财富盛宴正在脱轨。受访者要求匿名，因涉及诸多隐私细节。中国国家税务总局未回应置评请求。

The country’s richest people are facing a ticking clock, having been given until late October to pay bills on their overseas trusts without facing extra charges. That’s spread fear among the wealthy, who are quickly [trying to figure out](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-08-12/china-s-global-tax-hunt-is-sowing-chaos-and-confusion) how much they owe and how they can find the money to pay. Some Chinese citizens are rushing to get foreign passports, worried that Beijing will start imposing an exit tax on those moving abroad, people familiar with the matter say. Others are dumping stocks or taking out hefty loans overseas.  
中国最富有的群体正面临倒计时压力——他们必须在 10 月下旬前缴清海外信托账单才能避免额外费用。这引发了富豪阶层的恐慌，他们正紧急核算欠税金额并筹措资金。知情人士透露，部分中国公民正急于获取外国护照，担心北京将对移民海外者开征离境税。另一些人则抛售股票或在海外申请大额贷款。

Shu Ping, the co-founder of hotpot chain [Haidilao International Holding Ltd.](bbg://securities/6862%20HK%20Equity/DES), for example, sold around [$350 million of the company’s shares](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-14/china-s-tax-clampdown-raises-fears-of-founder-share-disposals) through a family trust. The move came after her family got hit with a surprise tax bill, according to people familiar with her situation. Haidilao didn’t respond to requests for comment.  
以火锅连锁品牌海底捞国际控股有限公司联合创始人舒萍为例，她通过家族信托出售了价值约 3.5 亿美元的公司股份。知情人士称，此举是在其家族突遭税务稽查后做出的。海底捞未回应置评请求。

A few stubborn tycoons are fighting back. Some are haggling with tax officials; others are considering legal fights if their tax bill seems unfair or unaffordable, according to a person familiar with their thinking. To circumvent local tax authorities, some wealthy Chinese are trying to play cities against one another. The controlling shareholder of a Guangzhou-based company served with a 100 million yuan ($15 million) tax bill threatened to relocate to Shanghai, ultimately persuading local officials to lower the tally to 5 million yuan, according to another person.  
少数顽固的富豪正发起反击。据知情人士透露，有人与税务官员讨价还价；另一些则在税单看似不公或难以承担时考虑诉诸法律。为规避地方税务部门，部分中国富人试图挑动城市间竞争。据另一位消息人士称，广州某企业控股股东在收到 1 亿元人民币（1500 万美元）税单后，以迁址上海相要挟，最终迫使当地官员将金额降至 500 万元。

One wealthy family asked an adviser to help them find a small bank to hold their money, preferably one that officials in Beijing hadn’t heard of. The family thinks bigger banks like [JPMorgan Chase & Co.](bbg://securities/JPM%20US%20Equity/FA) and [UBS Group AG](bbg://securities/UBSG%20SW%20Equity/FA) are too high-profile, says a person familiar with the discussions. Representatives for the two banks declined to comment.  
一个富裕家庭曾向顾问寻求帮助，希望找一家北京官员没听说过的小型银行来存放资金。据知情人士透露，该家族认为摩根大通和瑞银集团等大型银行过于引人注目。两家银行的代表均拒绝置评。

China’s tax grab comes at a time when the country’s economy is struggling under the weight of a prolonged [real estate crunch](https://www.bloomberg.com/latest/china-s-property-crisis), which has dealt a severe blow to the finances of local governments. Land sales, which once generated more than $1.1 trillion of revenue annually for these governments, are now forecast to hit less than a third of that.  
中国的税务严查正值该国经济在长期房地产低迷的重压下艰难前行之际，这场危机已对地方财政造成严重打击。土地出让金曾为地方政府带来每年逾 1.1 万亿美元的收入，如今预计将不足此数的三分之一。

![](https://assets.bwbx.io/images/users/iqjWHBFdfxIU/iCB_Mg6vA4Rw/v3/pidjEfPlU1QWZop3vfGKsrX.ke8XuWirGYh1PKgEw44kE/-1x-1.png)

The country is also planning to [spend almost $300 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-09/china-prepares-295-billion-plan-to-fund-nationwide-ai-buildout) over the next five years to build data centers, a key part of its ambition to match the US in the artificial intelligence race. That’s putting more pressure on the country’s creaky local governments to get their houses in order. “They are trying every possible channel to get more funds,” says [Henry Gao](https://faculty.smu.edu.sg/profile/henry-gao-2121), a law professor at Singapore Management University. “Everything from the lions, so to speak, to the mosquitoes.”  
中国还计划在未来五年投入近 3000 亿美元建设数据中心，这是其追赶美国人工智能雄心的关键一环。这给本就捉襟见肘的地方财政带来了更大压力。新加坡管理大学法学教授高树超表示："他们正尝试所有可能的渠道筹集资金，用俗话说就是'狮子蚊子一起抓'。"

Jaden Zhao, 46, who owns a textile company in Guangdong, couldn’t believe what he was hearing when he received an unexpected phone call from local officials in early August. Like many of his countrymen, Zhao had used overseas brokerage accounts for years to trade stocks in international markets. These trades are officially subject to tax within China, but for years officials didn’t enforce the rules. Apparently that had changed: The officials told Zhao he owed 9 million yuan in taxes linked to trading profits.  
46 岁的赵杰登在广东经营一家纺织公司，8 月初接到当地官员的意外来电时，他几乎不敢相信自己的耳朵。与许多同胞一样，赵多年来一直使用海外经纪账户在国际市场交易股票。这些交易按规定需在中国境内纳税，但多年来官方并未严格执行。如今情况显然变了：官员告知赵杰登，他因交易利润需补缴 900 万元税款。

Zhao, who says the amount is way beyond what he thinks he should owe, has pored over his trading history and hired a lawyer to help him get the records of trading accounts he’d already closed. He wants to reconstruct the numbers step by step and compare his calculations with those of the tax authorities. “Right now,” he says, “my biggest hope is that they got the numbers wrong.”  
赵某表示，通知金额远超其认为应缴数额。他仔细核对了交易记录，并聘请律师协助调取已注销账户的交易流水，试图逐步还原数据并与税务部门的计算结果进行比对。"眼下，"他说道，"我最大的希望是他们算错了数字。"

Tax officials in city or provincial departments across China have issued a raft of demands through letters and phone calls. Although some people like Zhao have been given specific numbers, others have received much vaguer messages. Instead, they’ve been asked to estimate what they think they owe, giving tax officials a starting point for negotiations, the people say.  
中国各地市级和省级税务部门通过信函和电话发出了一系列补税要求。据知情人士透露，虽然像赵某这样的部分人士收到了具体补税金额通知，但更多人收到的信息则含糊得多——税务部门要求他们自行估算应缴税款，以此作为协商的起点。

Some analysts believe the government may even introduce an inheritance tax, bringing an end to the quirk of a Communist country — at least in name — that has for decades not taken any taxes on such wealth transfers. (In contrast, the US charges an estate tax rate of as much as 40%, generating big business for tax advisers who help rich Americans lower their bills.)  
一些分析人士认为，政府甚至可能开征遗产税，这将终结这个至少在名义上的共产主义国家数十年来不对财富传承征税的特例。（相比之下，美国征收最高达 40%的遗产税，这为帮助富豪减税的顾问们创造了巨大商机。）

For decades, Chinese tax officials maintained a loose status quo, based as much on what was tolerated as on what the letter of the law was. For instance, China’s government limits its citizens from taking more than $50,000 out of the country each year, but tax officials didn’t appear concerned that many had millions of dollars of assets overseas. And though these citizens officially had to pay capital gains for overseas stocks, few did. “So long as they’d met their tax revenue target, they weren’t that interested in enforcing the tax law,” explains Christine Wong, a senior fellow at the National University of Singapore’s East Asian Institute. “But now they’re really short of money.”  
数十年来，中国税务部门维持着一种宽松的现状，其执法尺度既取决于法律规定，更取决于容忍限度。例如，中国政府规定公民每年向境外转移资金不得超过 5 万美元，但税务官员似乎并不在意许多人拥有数百万美元的海外资产。尽管这些公民按规定需为海外股票缴纳资本利得税，但实际缴纳者寥寥无几。"只要完成了税收目标，他们对执法并不太感兴趣，"新加坡国立大学东亚研究所高级研究员克里斯汀·黄解释道，"但现在他们真的缺钱了。"

China’s central and local governments [spent $1.9 trillion more](bbg://news/stories/TB8RLBT9NJLV) than they brought in last year, according to official data. Desperate for an alternative to their lost cash cow, they’ve turned their attention back to the wealthy. Asked whether the effort to collect taxes from wealthy individuals’ overseas investments might finally make it possible to pay salaries again, a deputy party secretary in a city in the central Henan province — who hasn’t been paid anything for a year — demurs. Given the government’s debt load, that money won’t be coming to payroll, the official says.  
官方数据显示，去年中国中央和地方政府财政收支缺口达 1.9 万亿美元。在失去传统财源后，各级政府将征税重点重新转向高净值人群。当被问及对富豪海外投资征税是否能让财政恢复发放薪资时，河南省某市一位已一年未领工资的市委副书记未予正面回应。该官员表示，鉴于政府债务负担，这部分税收不会用于补发工资。

![](https://assets.bwbx.io/images/users/iqjWHBFdfxIU/ikHNj3H__WP0/v3/pidjEfPlU1QWZop3vfGKsrX.ke8XuWirGYh1PKgEw44kE/-1x-1.png)

Some wealthy Chinese are now taking out loans backed by their overseas assets to help them pay tax bills, say people familiar with the matter. Tax loans for Chinese clients are set to become a rising source of revenue for banks like Goldman Sachs Group Inc. and JPMorgan, the people say. Representatives for the banks declined to comment. Some companies are offering loans to their founders to help them pay tax bills, according to a person familiar with their plans.  
据知情人士透露，部分中国富豪正通过抵押海外资产获取贷款来缴纳税款。这些人士表示，面向中国客户提供的税务贷款将成为高盛集团和摩根大通等银行日益增长的收入来源。两家银行的代表均拒绝置评。另有知情人士称，部分企业正在向创始人提供贷款以协助其完成纳税。

One headache for taxpayers is how to get money back inside the country, an unusual position for a generation of people who’ve focused on getting their money out. China’s annual transfer limit, designed to defend the country’s currency, applies in both directions. Chengdu, Zhuhai and other cities have created so-called green channels to help taxpayers quickly move more than $50,000 into the country to settle their bills, according to people familiar with the matter.  
令纳税人头疼的问题是如何将资金转回国内——对于这代习惯于将资金转移出境的人来说，这种处境实属罕见。中国为维护本币稳定而设的年度换汇限额是双向适用的。据知情人士透露，成都、珠海等城市已开通所谓"绿色通道"，帮助纳税人快速转入超过 5 万美元资金用于缴纳税款。

In May, the tax department of Shijiazhuang, a bustling city in the northern Hebei province, said it had assembled a special team to help taxpayers wire foreign currencies for tax payment. It gave the example of a “Mr. Liu,” who reportedly paid around $149,000 of taxes linked to his overseas stock trades by transferring money from a Hong Kong dollar account.  
今年五月，河北省会石家庄市税务局宣布成立专项工作组，协助纳税人办理跨境缴税外汇汇款。该局以"刘先生"为例，称其通过港币账户转账缴纳了约 14.9 万美元的境外股票交易相关税款。

Unsurprisingly, Chinese tycoons have asked for further clarity on the rules. One gray area is the use of offshore trusts that were established before Jan. 1, 2023, a date that was explicitly mentioned in a recent rule change, but which some high-net-worth individuals worry isn’t a real cut-off. Assets transferred into trusts before that date could still be taxed, they say they fear.  
中国富豪们要求政策进一步透明化并不令人意外。其中一个模糊地带涉及 2023 年 1 月 1 日前设立的离岸信托——这个日期在近期法规修订中被明确提及，但部分高净值人士担忧这并非真正的截止线。他们表示，即便是在该日期前转入信托的资产仍可能面临征税风险。

Wealthy Chinese individuals are also asking tax officials how far they’re looking back, whether an annual loss could offset profits and whether a lump-sum payment could cut the bill, according to people familiar with their plans. Compliance is further stalled by missing paper trails, as many banks delete records after seven years.  
据知情人士透露，中国的高净值人群正在向税务官员咨询追溯稽查的时间范围、年度亏损能否抵扣盈利以及一次性缴清能否减免税款等问题。由于许多银行会在七年后删除交易记录，凭证缺失进一步阻碍了合规进程。

The sharpest disputes center on what disgruntled taxpayers see as outdated valuation benchmarks. They’re pushing back against tax bills that appear to value their assets at peak levels, including for listed stocks that have since tumbled in value. Since there’s no standard valuation model for the tax bills, the final tally can be hammered out in private discussions, the people say.  
最激烈的争议集中在纳税人认为陈旧的估值基准上。这些心怀不满的纳税者正在抵制那些似乎按照资产峰值估值的税单——包括对那些已大幅贬值的上市公司股票的估值。知情人士透露，由于税务评估缺乏标准估值模型，最终税额往往通过私下协商敲定。

China’s decades-long economic rise may be the greatest period of wealth creation in human history, giving a huge bonus to the world’s financial institutions. Private bankers, who spend their time wooing the world’s richest people, may benefit from a short-term boost, as their clients scramble to find the money to pay bills. A banker in Hong Kong says all his time is being spent on taxes, adding that this is the busiest summer he’s ever seen. His firm is advising clients on selling blocks of shares in their own companies or using private jets or stock portfolios as collateral to take out loans.  
中国长达数十年的经济崛起可能是人类历史上最伟大的财富创造时期，为全球金融机构带来了巨大红利。私人银行家们终日周旋于全球富豪之间，短期内或将迎来业务激增——因为他们的客户正急于筹措资金缴纳税款。一位香港银行家表示，他所有时间都花在了处理税务问题上，并称这是他经历过最忙碌的夏天。其所在机构正建议客户通过抛售所持公司大宗股票，或以私人飞机、股票组合作为抵押品申请贷款来应对税务压力。

But the longer-term outlook is dreary. China’s tax grab could lead to as much as $100 billion being charged on assets held by wealthy people in Hong Kong, according to an extreme scenario presented by analysts at Barclays. A tax lawyer in the country estimated the haul would likely be more in the range of $15 billion to $25 billion. Whatever the numbers are, the squeeze will dent the appeal of setting up trusts overseas for the country’s richest people.  
但长期前景并不乐观。根据巴克莱分析师提出的极端情景预测，中国的税务稽查可能导致香港富人持有的资产面临高达 1000 亿美元的补税。一位国内税务律师估计，实际追缴金额更可能在 150 亿至 250 亿美元之间。无论具体数字如何，这种挤压都将削弱中国超级富豪在海外设立信托的吸引力。

Many wealthy Chinese say they worry that submitting tax filings this year will expose sensitive information and raise questions about how they got so much money outside China, according to people familiar with their thinking. That means the tax grab could lead to a wider backlash against offshore wealth.  
据知情人士透露，许多中国富豪担忧今年提交的税务申报会暴露敏感信息，并引发关于他们如何将巨额资金转移至境外的质疑。这意味着此次税务行动可能引发针对离岸财富更广泛的反制浪潮。

China’s rich may have a long wait before the dust settles. Some of them who offered to pay big lump sums to settle tax bills have unofficially been asked to spread payments over five years, say people familiar with the negotiations. Local tax officials, keen to hit performance targets for years to come, are certain of one thing: They want the boon to last.  
中国富豪可能需经历漫长等待才能尘埃落定。熟悉谈判情况的知情人士称，部分提出大额一次性补税的富豪被非正式要求分五年缴清。渴望达成未来数年绩效目标的地方税务官员们确信一点：他们希望这场红利持续更久。
